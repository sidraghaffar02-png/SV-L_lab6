| Constraint ID | Constraint in Simple English                                                                            | Why the Constraint is Necessary                                                 |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| C1            | The barrier must not open while a train is present at the crossing.                                     | It prevents road traffic from entering the crossing while the train is passing. |
| C2            | The barrier must close when an approaching train is detected.                                           | It stops road traffic before the train reaches the crossing.                    |
| C3            | Warning lights must turn ON when a train approaches.                                                    | It warns drivers that a train is coming.                                        |
| C4            | The audible alarm must turn ON when a train approaches.                                                 | It gives an additional warning to road users.                                   |
| C5            | The barrier must remain closed while the train is passing through the crossing.                         | It prevents vehicles from entering the crossing during train movement.          |
| C6            | The barrier can open only after the system confirms that the train has completely cleared the crossing. | It ensures that it is safe for road traffic to proceed.                         |
| C7            | If the train sensor fails, the system must not allow an unsafe barrier opening.                         | It prevents incorrect sensor information from causing an unsafe situation.      |
| C8            | If the barrier fails, the system must activate an appropriate safety response.                          | A failed barrier could allow vehicles onto the railway crossing.                |
| C9            | If communication with the control center is lost, the system must continue operating safely.            | Communication failure should not make the crossing unsafe.                      |
| C10           | During an emergency, the system must enter a safe state.                                                | It helps protect people during emergency conditions.                            |
